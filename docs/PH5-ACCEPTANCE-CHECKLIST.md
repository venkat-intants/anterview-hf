**Notes.**
- **Small groups.** A rate, median or mean whose population is under 5 is marked *too few to compare*. Pipeline figures keep their value: a rate still shows its numerator and denominator, and a rate, a median and a mean each show the withheld figure on hover. Check-in coverage, retention and performance values are blanked below 5 — nothing on hover either — and for retention and performance the numerator is blanked too. When any group's retention or performance is blanked, it is blanked in every group, so it cannot be recovered by subtraction.
- **Live figures.** Definitions are frozen; figures are recalculated from the records each time, so they can move when a record does (an offer withdrawn after a hire).
- **Only the current version is calculated.** Every past version stays identifiable in the lock and in `GET /hr/metrics/definitions`, and a figure resolves its own version; the glossary lists the versions in force. Calculating an old version side by side would need `compute.py` extended, when the first version 2 is published.
- **Meaning changes** from the pre-PH5 roll-up, each stated in the metric's change note: *interviewed* now means an AI interview completed or a human scorecard submitted (not the ledger status, and no longer an AI scorecard alone — which is what the HR console's Interviewed tile used to count); *sat exam* no longer counts another application's exam, and now also counts an assessment recorded with no exam attempt behind it; *hired* counts only hires that stand; *time to hire* counts only hires that stand and starts at the application's `created_at` rather than the ledger's first move to `new`, which some applications never had. The console's Interviewed and Exam-taken counts, and the pipeline panel's bars, follow these definitions now, so both can read higher than before.

**Notes.**
- **Nothing here is about a person's competence.** A gap says an interviewer scored the same candidates differently from the rest of the panel; it does not say who was right. The screen says so, interviewers are never ranked, and the words *bias*, *harsh*, *lenient* and *outlier* are banned from the code as well as the copy.
- **Small groups.** Nothing is published from fewer than 5 distinct applications, per interviewer and per criterion cell, and a panel baseline also needs at least two interviewers. A suppressed row keeps only its population sizes; the drill-down refuses anything the summary withheld.
- **You do not see what you still owe.** Candidates you have an open scorecard for are left out of everything this screen shows, until you submit it.
- **Governed, but not a C2 metric.** Calibration's unit is one judgement (an application × round × competency × interviewer), not one application, so it cannot be a metric over the C2 cohort engine. Its method and every threshold are locked in the same file instead, as `calibration:interviewer_calibration@1`. The outcome view IS the C2 engine, grouped by a new governed dimension.
- **Score bands** are fixed edges on the 1-5 scale (under 3, 3 to under 4, 4 and above, and no human scorecard), never quantiles — so the boundary is governed rather than moving with the data. Only human interviewer scorecards are ever used: AI interview scores are deleted after 90 days and are structurally excluded.

**Notes.**
- **Derived, not stored.** There is no evidence-edges table: every node and edge is computed from the rows the existing screens already show, each time the trail is opened. That is what makes erasure, retention, redaction and consent withdrawal inherited — they appear on the very next read, with nothing of this package's own to keep in sync. The cost is that the graph is rebuilt per read rather than queried as stored relationships.
- **"Available when this was decided" is about time, and only time.** The system can show what existed when someone decided; it cannot show what they read or relied on, so nothing is ever labelled *supported by* or *based on*. Evidence recorded later is listed separately, and evidence corrected after the decision is flagged rather than absorbed.
- **One field the graph withholds itself.** A decision's free-text rationale is not redacted at its source (AR-5), so the trail hides it once the candidate is erased.
- **Never shown here:** interviewer notes, 90-day check-ins, accommodations, coding source and similarity, the audit log, offer compensation, screening answer text, AI prose and candidate contact details — each named on screen with its reason.
